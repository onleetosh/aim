# A.I.M

Many people set meaningful goals but struggle to turn them into consistent action. When a goal feels too large, unclear, or overwhelming, it can be difficult to know where to begin or what to do next. This often leads to procrastination, loss of focus, and stalled progress, leaving important tasks unfinished or taking longer than expected to complete.

**A.I.M.** is a full-stack, modern Kanban-style task management application built to help users break ambitious goals into actionable tasks, organize their work, and track progress through Agile-inspired workflows. By turning larger goals into manageable steps, A.I.M. helps users stay focused, build momentum, and move steadily toward completion.

What makes A.I.M. different is its combination of goal planning and Agile-inspired task management. It gives users a clear structure for transforming overwhelming objectives into achievable actions and maintaining steady progress over time.
